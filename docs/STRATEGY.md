# Target fit and progression

Creators own Minimum/Ideal goals, numeric weights, perk routes, flexible picks, and tags. Bro Planner includes ten original, editable-by-copy starter templates with approachable level-11 natural-stat goals. They may be suboptimal and are starting guides, not build-power ratings or tactical guarantees.

## Formula

For each targeted stat, remaining Ideal shortfall is `weight × max(Ideal − endpoint, 0) / referenceRoll`. The fixed ordinary-roll references in HP/Resolve/Fatigue/Initiative/MAtk/RAtk/MDef/RDef order are **3/3/3/4/2/3/2/3**. These units describe training increments, not equivalent combat benefits. Actual offers, talents and native final rounding determine endpoint gains; they never change the references.

Choose one legal allocation minimizing the sum of these shortfalls at average rolls. Only an exact tie prefers the smaller weighted Minimum shortfall, measured in the same units. Minimum is a review floor and tie preference, not a hard requirement or a half-score anchor. Equal Minimum/Ideal uses the same rule. Negative values retain their full deficit; zero targets are met by nonnegative values. Surplus above Ideal never contributes. Blank ranges and weight 0 are ignored; a build with no positive-weight targets is unrated.

**Goal gap** divides the optimized weighted Ideal shortfall by a fixed **10**, the maximum allowed preference weight. Minimum gap uses the same divisor. Lower is closer; zero means all Ideals fit at average rolls. Weights use a shared 0–10 importance scale across builds. Multiplying every weight by the same positive factor preserves allocation and advice, but scales both gaps by that factor and can change cross-build order. Adding completed goals at any allowed weight adds no gap and cannot improve ranking. Tightening a goal cannot reduce its optimized gap. Easier goals and lower importance still yield smaller gaps: this ranks stated preferences, not combat power, probability or level-ups needed. Minimum gap breaks ranking ties, followed by source and stable build ID. All decisions and ranking use unrounded values; only the UI rounds to two decimals.

Version 1.1.0 applies this rule app-wide, including existing libraries, starters, BL1/BL2 imports and saved plans. The numeric targets, weights, routes and saved snapshots are unchanged, but rankings, forecasts and advice can change. Earlier versions interpret those same numbers using the former satisfaction curve. There is one scoring rule and no conversion workflow or schema change. Legacy plans without saved Ideals remain unrated and use their retained Minimum for advice.

The finite optimizer uses dynamic programming over shared normal, earned veteran, and obtainable Gifted budgets. A joint witness reaching all capped individual maxima is already optimal. When every attribute remains in one linear goal segment and its native endpoints are certified affine over the finite domain, constant marginal Ideal and Minimum values give the same optimum directly. These shortcuts use the same objective. Nonlinear native rounding and threshold crossings retain the general optimizer. For larger domains, a legal provisional allocation supplies a lower bound and nonnegative resource prices supply upper bounds. Only choices and continuations proven unable to beat that allocation are pruned, with magnitude-aware slack for native floating-point arithmetic; the provisional allocation is never substituted for the exact result.

The eight-stat forecast reconstructs the winning allocation and fills unused slots deterministically to exactly three distinct attributes per row. Filler does not increase an ignored attribute's importance. Learned perks never break goal-gap ties. The forecast is an allocation witness, not a prediction of player choices or revealed rolls.

## Stat basis and finite growth

Targets use permanent natural stats including Colossus and Fortified Mind, the only two native perks that scale a tracked attribute, before gear and Dodge. An acquired stat perk raises the brother's current value; one the tracked build still plans raises the reachable maximum and forecast only, and is credited by a perk mark beside that Potential value. Lasting traits, origin effects, injuries, and already allocated Gifted gains count. Raw growth is added before native final rounding and sign-dependent multipliers. Initiative retains its native permanent Stamina deduction. Temporary morale, fatigue, damage, and equipment condition do not affect fit.

Growth uses safe native ranges and visible talents, never future offers. Ordinary growth stops at level 11; Manhunter Indebted stop at level 7. Already earned veteran rows use +1; future veteran levels are not invented. An obtainable, unlearned planned Gifted adds one row of three maximum ordinary gains without talent bonuses. Student's single refund obeys native point availability. Uncertain pending row types or required growth produce an unrated result.

The compact panel compares current permanent values with saved Ideals. Green means already met. Red requires that even investing every remaining row in that attribute with maximum rolls cannot reach its Ideal, within the displayed horizon. Yellow means development is needed; it does not promise eventual success. Unknown growth never supplies a red bound. A separate joint message reports when individually possible goals cannot all fit within the same maximum-roll budget. A shortfall at average rolls alone is not impossibility.

Current-offer advice enumerates all 56 triples of the eight visible rolls. It prefers the smallest remaining Ideal shortfall, then Minimum shortfall, then immediate weighted Ideal progress. Ignored attributes and already-met Ideals are omitted; the player fills any remaining choices. Selected unmet-Ideal picks remain recommended even when native rounding hides today's gain: that raw growth can be required for a later endpoint. All-met and entirely ignored builds give no stat recommendations. Unknown growth uses only that immediate tie-break and is labelled provisional. Advice never spends points or modifies the actor.

## Perks and effects

A community route lists up to twenty unique perks in creator priority order. Tiers may be skipped: the picks needed to reach a perk's unlock count are filler picks, any perks including flexible ones, taken when no listed perk is yet unlockable. The minimum picks for a set of perks is `max(n, max_k(u_k − spent + n − k))` over its ascending unlock counts. Guidance compares that for the remaining mandatory perks with the brother's remaining points (plus one when an unowned Student in the route can be bought, counting its own pick) and reports filler picks or an exact over-budget shortfall. The budget is advice, never a save rule. Flex marks alternates. Manual off-route acquisitions can fill unacquired flex positions on a guidance copy; mandatory and already learned picks remain. Library edits, evaluation, and refresh cannot rewrite saved intent. Perk budget shortfalls are shown separately from stat fit; equipment/playstyle tags describe creator intent and do not certify a loadout.

Schema 5 snapshots the definition including all eight weights. Schemas 1–4 (before 0.5) are no longer read; such a brother has no plan until one is tracked again. Template or library changes never update saved snapshots. No retired catalog is consulted to recover unsaved Ideals. Unknown future schemas remain untouched.

The preference rule cannot infer action affordability, exposure, hit floors or survival breakpoints from natural-stat goals. For example, a low-weight Fatigue offer that enables another mastered-cleaver attack can still lose to higher-weight HP/attack/defence progress. Perk-route feasibility stays separate from stat gap; a blocked route is not certified by a good score.

Current learned effects retain the native readouts: Dodge `max(0, floor(current Initiative × .15))`; Nimble HP reduction `100 × (1 − acquired Nimble chance)`; Battle Forged armour reduction `(remaining head + body armour) × .05` percentage points. Percentages round to tenths. These individual current contributions do not forecast equipment, combined protection, or future perks.

Mechanics were inspected against Steam build 23856902 / Battle Brothers 1.5.2.3. The standalone Squirrel runner and synthetic fixtures do not certify game-VM behavior, tactical success, save safety, or UI fit.
